Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How will my donation be used?

90% of your donation goes directly to program services, with only 10% allocated to administrative costs. We provide detailed annual reports showing exactly how funds are distributed across our various initiatives.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes, Generosity Hub is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You will receive a receipt via email immediately after your donation that serves as your tax documentation.

Can I cancel or modify my recurring donation?

Absolutely. You can modify or cancel your recurring donation at any time by contacting our donor support team at support@generosityhub.org or by calling (555) 123-4567. Changes take effect before the next scheduled donation.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and bank transfers. All payment processing is secure and encrypted. We do not store your full payment details on our servers.
